Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

MINISAVE respects your privacy. This policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you use our platform and how that information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, MINISAVE may process information such as your name, email address, account details, savings goals, deposit requests, withdrawal requests and transaction records.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to operate customer accounts, display savings activity, process requests, provide customer support, maintain security and improve our services.

3. Data Storage and Security

Account and transaction information may be stored using our database service provider, Supabase. We use available security controls to help protect information. However, no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customers' personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the platform, comply with applicable law or protect users and MINISAVE.

5. Payments and Transactions

Payment information and transaction references may be processed or recorded when customers make deposit or withdrawal requests. Do not share passwords, PINs or one-time verification codes with anyone claiming to represent MINISAVE.

6. Data Retention

We may retain account and transaction records for as long as reasonably necessary for platform operations, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations.

7. Your Choices

You may contact MINISAVE to ask questions about your personal information or request assistance with your account. Some records may need to be retained where required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

MINISAVE is not intended for children who are not legally able to enter into the relevant financial arrangements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the Contact MINISAVE page.
